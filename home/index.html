Welcome to the official site of Heraklion Bridge Club!

Our bridge club is located in Heraklion of Crete in Greece and is an official member of the Hellenic Bridge Federation.

You are welcome to participate in our games, Wednesdays and Sundays (Mondays during July and August), at 19.30, whether you have a partner or not!
You should always contact us in advance to make sure that we enlist you in the tournament.
